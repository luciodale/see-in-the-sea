# RULES 2025

## 16th SEE IN THE SEA INTERNATIONAL UNDERWATER PHOTOCONTEST ORTONAMARE 2025 <span style='font-style: italic'> </br> MEMORIAL DAVID SALVATORI </span>

The Contest is open to all underwater photographers and is free-themed. The sections of the Contest are as follows:

**Wide Angle (WA):**  
The color image must represent the underwater environment or the species inhabiting it through the use of wide-angle lenses or add-ons. Wide shots and backlighting are allowed, and human presence may be included as a complementary element. The photographer is free to interpret the theme according to their own compositional taste, using any technique. Split-shot images are also permitted, provided that the underwater portion is not less than 50%.

**Macro:**  
The color image must capture entire aquatic subjects or details of subjects or underwater life that highlight the best aesthetic taste through composition and color choices. The images must be taken exclusively with macro lenses; fisheye lenses for close-up macro are excluded. Any technique is allowed, provided that the main subject of the image represents the theme.

**Black and White:**  
All free-themed underwater images in black and white taken with wide-angle or macro lenses are allowed.

**Mediterranean Portfolio:**  
One or two series of 3 color or B/W images, highlighting the environment and fauna typical of the Mediterranean Sea. Each portfolio must be composed as follows: one Macro, one WA, and one of free choice. Identical or similar Macro or WA images to those submitted in the other sections are not permitted (see ref. Art.1). Authors submitting two series must indicate with a title or other identifying form to which Portfolio each image belongs.

## FILE SUBMISSION

**Art. 1)**  
Authors may submit a maximum of 2 files per section and 2 series for the Mediterranean Portfolio. The same photo cannot be submitted in multiple sections. To participate, it is necessary to log in from **01/10/2025 to 31/12/2025** to the official contest page [www.seeintheseauw.com](http://www.seeintheseauw.com) and access the section dedicated to photo submission. No extensions for submission deadlines will be granted.

To submit works, registration on [www.seeintheseauw.com](http://www.seeintheseauw.com) via the **Enter the contest** button in the navigation bar is required. By registering, consent to data processing for purposes strictly related to site functionality is considered implicit. Once registration is completed, access will be automatic, and the user will be redirected to the submission section, where they can upload and manage their contest photos and pay the entry fee.

For each successfully uploaded file, the system will automatically send a confirmation email to the email address provided during registration.

For any technical issues, contact Lucio D'Alessandro at **lucio.dalessa@gmail.com**.

Each work must:

- Be in JPG format (the only accepted format).
- Have a file size between 5Mb and 10Mb.
- Have the following dimensions: 30×40 or 30×45 at 200 dpi.
- Have a title.
- Contain exif data as proof of the category of submission. Any information that could identify the author must be removed. Exif data will not be accessible to the jury during evaluation, but in case of need, only by the organization.

**Art. 2)**  
For digital images, the following are permitted:

- A maximum crop of 20% of the original image area.
- Limited use of the clone stamp to clean small defects.
- Small adjustments to brightness, contrast, color temperature, sharpness, and saturation.
- Photomontages or image overlays are not allowed.
- Multiple exposures are allowed only if permitted by the camera used and provided they generate a single RAW file.
- Images processed with AI (Artificial Intelligence) are not allowed.

Images must be free of frames, mats, watermarks, logos, or signatures that may identify the photographer.

Images taken through underwater portholes, aquariums, and swimming pools are not allowed.

Images showing damaged seabeds, frightened or stressed animals, or unnatural situations will not be admitted, except when intended to document or denounce negative human impacts.

The organization, in agreement with the jury, reserves the right to exclude images that, during verification, do not comply with Art. 2.

For all classified images, the RAW file will be requested without exception.

The jury’s decision is final and will be based on technical, naturalistic, artistic, and documentary aspects of the image. Images with significant technical or artistic shortcomings, even if with high naturalistic content, may receive a negative evaluation.

**Art. 3)**  
As reimbursement of expenses, an entry fee is required:

- €20.00 for those registering in one category only.
- €30.00 for those registering in multiple categories.

Payment may be made via **Stripe** after uploading the works in the chosen categories.

In case of problems with payment through Stripe, contact the organizational secretariat at **info@ortonasub.com** or **info@ortonamare.org**, or Mr. Fabrizio Pompilio at **+393803919121**.

**Art. 4)**  
The 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winners in each section will be awarded. Each participant may win only one prize offered by the sponsors. In case of multiple wins by the same participant, priority will be given to the prize of greater value.

If an author wins in multiple sections, they will receive the trophy for the highest placement, with an additional plaque indicating other results.

**NOTE:** At the sole discretion of the Jury, awards may be assigned, regardless of category and theme, also to photos classified as "MENTIONED."

**Art. 5)**  
During the Award Ceremony, the organization will present all winning photos, the mentioned ones, and those deemed of particular technical and emotional interest by the Jury.

Winning, mentioned, and most significant photos will be published on the official website [seeintheseauw.com](http://www.seeintheseauw.com) and on the Contest’s Facebook and Instagram pages at the same time as the official rankings are published.

**Art. 6)**  
Works will be admitted to the Contest only and exclusively after payment of the entry fee referred to in Art. 3.
